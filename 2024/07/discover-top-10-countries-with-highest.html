Welcome back to our awesome articles! After the success of our black-and-white series, many of you asked us to explore the countries with the highest percentage of white residents outside of Europe and Scandinavia. Yes, Scandinavia is part of Europe, but some people on the internet like to nitpick. Today, we're diving into the fascinating stats about these countries.


What Defines "White"?

For this article, we've used people of European origin to define the term "white." The data comes from the CIA website and the respective country's statistics where available. Ready? Let's dive in.


10. South Africa

Surprising, right? South Africa has a complex history of racial segregation known as apartheid, which ended in the 1990s. The country is home to about 60 million people, with 9% being considered white.


9. Mexico

Known for its vibrant culture and stunning beaches, Mexico also has a significant white population. Despite common stereotypes, about 10% of its 127 million residents are white.


8. Colombia

Colombia, famous for its coffee and emeralds, has a white population of about 20%. This South American gem boasts beautiful landscapes from the Caribbean coast to the Andes mountains.


7. Venezuela

Despite its current economic and political challenges, Venezuela has a rich cultural heritage and a white population of 43% among its 28 million residents.


6. Brazil

Brazil's history of European colonization and immigration during World War II has led to a white population of 47% out of 214 million residents.


5. Canada

The Great White North lives up to its name with 70% of its 38 million residents being white. Canada’s multicultural cities and breathtaking landscapes make it a unique blend of cultures.


4. United States

With a white population of 75%, the United States is a melting pot of cultures and histories, shaped by its European colonization and immigration.


3. Chile

Known for its stunning landscapes and diverse culture, Chile has a white population of 88% among its 20 million residents. From the Atacama Desert to Patagonia, Chile offers a rich cultural experience.


2. Australia

Originally a British penal colony, Australia now boasts a white population of 92% among its 26 million residents. Its unique wildlife and vast landscapes make it a fascinating place to explore.


1. Argentina

The biggest surprise on our list, Argentina has a white population of 97% out of its nearly 46 million residents. This South American country is known for its European influences and vibrant culture.
