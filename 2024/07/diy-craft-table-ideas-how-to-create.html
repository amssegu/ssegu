 DIY Craft Table Ideas: How to Create Your Own Cube Storage Craft Table

Creating a DIY cube storage craft table is an excellent way to add functionality and organization to your crafting space. With a crafting table customized to your specific needs, you can store all your crafting materials in an easily accessible and organized way. Here's a guide on how to create your own cube storage craft table with helpful tips and ideas to make the process easier and more fun.




  • Choose Your Cube Storage Organizer

    The first step in building your DIY craft table is to select the appropriate size and style of cube storage organizer. These are available in various configurations, from 4-cube to 9-cube sets or more. When choosing the cube organizer, consider how much storage space you need and the size of the area where the craft table will be placed.

  • Decide on the Tabletop Material

    For the tabletop, you can choose materials such as plywood, MDF, or even an old door. If you're looking for an affordable and sturdy option, plywood or MDF would be ideal. Make sure the size of the tabletop is at least an inch or two larger than the cube organizer on all sides, giving you a more spacious working surface.

  • Customize the Tabletop

    Once you've chosen the material for your tabletop, you can customize it to suit your taste or decor style. Consider painting it in a color that complements your room or using adhesive vinyl for a quick and easy update. You can also apply a layer of chalkboard paint for an added functional element where you can jot down ideas or to-do lists right on the table surface.

  • Attach the Tabletop to the Cubes

    To assemble your craft table, place the cube organizer in your desired location. Carefully place the tabletop on the top of the cube units. You can secure it by using screws or strong adhesive to ensure it stays in place. If you plan to move the table occasionally, consider attaching Velcro strips between the tabletop and the cubes for a secure yet adjustable connection.

  • Add Custom Organization Options

    Inside the cubes, use custom or store-bought storage options like baskets, bins, or drawer inserts. This can help categorize and separate your supplies, making them easier to find when you need them. Consider what kinds of crafts you do and set up your storage to accommodate items specific to those activities, like rolls for paper crafts, small compartments for beadwork, or hooks for fabrics.

  • Consider Mobility

    If you want to make your craft table more versatile, consider adding casters to the bottom of the cube storage organizer. This will allow you to move it around easily, whether it's for cleaning, reorienting your workspace, or adjusting your crafting area to better suit your needs.

  • Add Additional Lighting

    Good lighting is crucial for any crafting space. You can opt to add a lamp on the tabletop, or for a more integrated solution, install under-cabinet lighting beneath the tabletop to illuminate your work area. This can be particularly helpful for detailed work or when working in a room with limited natural light.

  • Include a Comfortable Crafting Chair

    Since you'll be spending a lot of time at your craft table, choose a chair that is comfortable and supports a healthy posture. Consider a chair with adjustable height and lumbar support to accommodate long hours of sitting and crafting.

  • Create a Power Supply Setup

    Make sure your crafting table is set up near a power outlet to accommodate all your crafting needs. If your chosen spot lacks sufficient outlets, install a power strip on one side of the table or underneath the tabletop for an accessible and organized way to power your craft tools and devices.

  • Accessorize and Personalize

    Finally, make the space your own by adding personal touches such as artwork, inspirational quotes, or a small plant. These small additions will not only brighten up the space but also make it more inviting and inspiring for your crafting sessions.

Building your own cube storage craft table is not only a fun project but also a practical decision that can greatly improve your efficiency and enjoyment in your crafting. By following these steps, you can customize your workspace to perfectly suit your needs and express your unique creative style.
