Welcome to ssegu, the place where people come to get inspired and educated about the world. Hello! If there’s one article you’ve been asking us to make every week, it’s this one. Initially, we aimed for a top ten ranking of the best business books, but to be completely honest, what works for some might not work for others. That’s why we decided to list 15 books we found valuable at the start of our journey. This isn’t a top ten list but a collection of books filled with nuggets of gold that could benefit anyone looking to expand their library and knowledge.


We believe that depending on where you are in your journey, you should adjust your reading material to increase your chances of moving forward with your current projects. The following list covers a wide range of ways of thinking about life and business, so pick the ones that will impact your life the most right now. We’ve linked all the books mentioned in this article, so you can purchase them on Amazon if you wish. If you’re new here, welcome! Be sure to subscribe and follow us on our socials.


1. Think and Grow Rich by Napoleon Hill

When to Read: Right after high school you want to expand your horizons.

This book is the pillar on which all other business books were built. While its title and much of the text concern increased income, Hill insists the philosophy taught can help people succeed in any line of work. As the protégé of Andrew Carnegie, one of the richest people to ever live, Hill spent 20 years studying individuals who amassed personal fortunes to uncover their common traits.


2. The Secret by Rhonda Byrne

When to Read: When you've never read a business book but want to be successful.

Though we’ve said positive thinking alone isn't enough, this book is a great starting point for beginners. Focused exclusively on mindset, it’s perfect for those just starting to read self-help books.


3. Rich Dad Poor Dad by Robert Kiyosaki

When to Read: When you're ready to start your financial journey.

This book profoundly impacts how we view life and financial freedom, teaching the importance of cash flow and the difference between assets and liabilities. It’s widely regarded as one of the best business books ever written.


4. The Intelligent Investor by Benjamin Graham

When to Read: When considering investing in the stock market.

Graham, the mentor and inspiration of Warren Buffett, teaches the vital elements of value investing. If you want to venture into the stock market, this is an essential read.


5. Money: Master the Game by Tony Robbins

When to Read: When money starts coming in and you want to know what to do with it.

Tony Robbins leverages his connections to share strategies of the super-wealthy. This book simplifies the process of securing your financial future in an accessible way.


6. The 4-Hour Workweek by Tim Ferriss

When to Read: When you need a fresh start because life is passing you by.

This book promotes the idea of location independence, allowing you to travel, make money, and live life to the fullest. It’s a rare book that redefines conventional wisdom.


7. The Millionaire Fastlane by MJ DeMarco

When to Read: Immediately after reading The 4-Hour Workweek.

DeMarco’s less-known book actively dismisses popular business books and presents strong arguments and personal stories, perfect for those with a "go big or go home" mentality.


8. The Power of Habit by Charles Duhigg

When to Read: When you’re procrastinating all the time.

Duhigg’s book dives into the science of habits and how to alter them for personal gain, filled with fascinating stories and valuable insights.


9. Atlas Shrugged by Ayn Rand

When to Read: When you’re on holiday and need something to keep your mind busy.

Mark Cuban’s favorite book, this science fiction romance mystery novel outlines Rand’s philosophy on objectivism. It’s polarizing, but worth the read.


10. How to Win Friends and Influence People by Dale Carnegie

When to Read: When you feel you're never taken seriously and struggle to build relationships.

This classic breaks down human interaction fundamentals, teaching you how to handle people, make them interested in you, and bring them over to your way of thinking.


11. The $100 Startup by Chris Guillebeau

When to Read: When you want to start a business but feel you don't have enough money.

This book looks at how to establish a lucrative business with minimal startup costs and is filled with case studies and practical advice.


12. The Monk Who Sold His Ferrari by Robin Sharma

When to Read: When you feel that despite making a ton of money, you’re not happy.

Sharma’s book explores why money doesn’t bring happiness and what to do about it, perfect for those who have already achieved financial success but seek fulfillment.


13. The E-Myth Revisited by Michael E. Gerber

When to Read: If you’re struggling with your first business.

This classic walks you through the stages of a business, teaching the distinction between working on your business and in your industry.


14. The 10X Rule by Grant Cardone

When to Read: When your business is no longer growing.

Cardone’s book cuts to the chase with no fancy words, focusing on sales strategies to drive business growth.


15. Zero to One by Peter Thiel

When to Read: When you’re ready to disrupt an entire industry.

Thiel, the founder of PayPal and Palantir, offers insights on the future, the current state of startups, and potential industry disruptions.
