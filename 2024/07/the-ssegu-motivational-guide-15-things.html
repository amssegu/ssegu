The Ssegu Motivational Guide: 15 Things That Keep You Poor

we’re diving into a critical topic: 15 things that poor people waste money on, preventing them from moving forward financially.

SSEGUPost2024-07-30

Welcome to our ssegu motivational article! we bring you insightful content to improve your life. Today, we’re diving into a critical topic: 15 things that poor people waste money on, preventing them from moving forward financially.


We’re excited to have you back for another transformative article. This one might ruffle some feathers, but our goal is to highlight common financial mistakes that keep people stuck. As you go through this list, check to see if you’re guilty of any of these wasteful spending habits. If you’re new here, welcome! Be sure to subscribe and follow our socials.

Without further ado, here are 15 things poor people waste money on that hold them back in life:


1. Credit Cards

Credit cards are financial traps that can dig you deeper into debt. Poor people often max out credit cards on non-essential items, making it difficult to escape the debt cycle. Remember, debt means you’re no longer working for yourself until it’s repaid.


2. Glam and Beautification

Spending money on nails, haircuts, and other beauty treatments while neglecting essential needs is a common mistake. Prioritize fixing your life and finances before indulging in temporary beauty fixes.


3. Impulse Purchases

We’ve all been tempted by unnecessary purchases like phone cases, funky socks, and kitchen gadgets. Learning to differentiate between needs and wants can save you thousands of dollars annually.


4. Food Waste

The average person throws away a significant portion of their groceries. Plan your meals better to reduce food waste and save money.


5. Miracle Diets and Fitness Products

Miracle diets and fitness products are often scams. True fitness requires a commitment to healthy eating and regular exercise, not quick fixes sold on TV or social media.


6. Lucky Charms and Spiritual Items

Spending money on lucky charms and spiritual items won’t improve your finances. Save that money and invest it in something more productive.


7. Neglecting Health

Ignoring health issues can lead to costly medical bills down the line. Regular check-ups and preventive care are cheaper than emergency treatments.


8. Games and In-App Purchases

Many people waste money on games and in-app purchases. Instead, focus on productive activities that can enhance your life and financial situation.


9. The Latest Tech

Buying the latest tech gadgets every year is unnecessary and costly. Prioritize maintaining your current devices to save money.


10. Clubbing

Spending money at clubs may feel good temporarily, but it’s a financial drain. Save that money for more meaningful and lasting experiences.


11. Gambling

Gambling is a losing game for most people. Instead of buying lottery tickets or playing slots, invest your money wisely.


12. Smoking and Vaping

Smoking and vaping are expensive habits that can drain your finances. Quitting can save you thousands of dollars annually.


13. Bank Fees

Banks often charge various fees that can add up. Look for ways to minimize these fees and keep more of your money.


14. Taxes

Understanding and leveraging the tax system can save you money. Poor people often pay more in taxes because they don’t know how to optimize their finances.


15. Donations

While charity is admirable, ensure your donations are going to the right places. Some charities spend a significant portion of their funds on administrative costs rather than helping those in need.

Bonus Tip: Stop Trying to Look Rich

Faking wealth can drain your finances. Focus on growing your wealth instead of just looking rich. Invest wisely and make decisions that contribute to your financial growth.
