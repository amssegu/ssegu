Discover 21 Platforms to Sell AI Art and Make Passive Income

AI art, passive income, sell AI art, online art platforms, digital art marketplace, monetize AI art

SSEGUPost2024-06-05

The art world is undergoing a remarkable transformation with the advent of AI-generated art. This innovative form of digital creation leverages artificial intelligence to produce stunning and unique pieces that captivate audiences. As an artist or digital creator, tapping into this trend can open up new streams of passive income. Whether you're an experienced artist looking to diversify your income sources or a tech enthusiast exploring the potential of AI, knowing where to sell your AI art is crucial. This comprehensive guide explores 21 platforms where you can sell your AI-generated art and start making passive income.


Selling AI art online provides a platform to showcase your creativity while tapping into a growing market of collectors and art enthusiasts eager to own unique digital pieces. From traditional art marketplaces to cutting-edge NFT platforms, these sites offer various ways to monetize your AI art effectively. Let’s dive into these platforms and explore how you can get started.


1. Etsy

Overview: Etsy is a well-known marketplace for handmade, vintage, and unique items. Recently, it has become a popular platform for digital art, including AI-generated pieces.

Benefits:

  • Established customer base

  • Easy setup process

  • Low listing fees

Getting Started: Create an Etsy shop, upload your digital art, and set your prices. Utilize keywords and descriptions to make your listings more discoverable and attract buyers looking for unique digital art.


2. Redbubble

Overview: Redbubble is a print-on-demand platform that allows artists to upload their designs and sell them on various products like t-shirts, mugs, and phone cases.

Benefits:

  • No upfront costs

  • Wide range of products

  • International audience

Getting Started: Create a Redbubble account, upload your AI art, and choose the products you want to sell. Redbubble handles the production, shipping, and customer service, making it easy for you to focus on creating more art【source】.


3. Society6

Overview: Society6 is another print-on-demand service that focuses on home decor, art prints, and lifestyle products.

Benefits:

  • High-quality products

  • Support for various art forms

  • Active artist community

Getting Started: Sign up as an artist on Society6, upload your artwork, and choose the products for your designs. Promote your shop on social media and other channels to attract customers and drive sales.


4. ArtStation

Overview: ArtStation is a platform for artists to showcase and sell their digital art, including AI-generated pieces. It's widely used by professional artists and designers.

Benefits:

  • Professional portfolio features

  • Large artist and buyer community

  • Opportunities for freelance work

Getting Started: Create an ArtStation account, set up your portfolio, and start selling your art prints and digital downloads. Utilize the community and networking opportunities to connect with other artists and potential buyers【source】.


5. DeviantArt

Overview: DeviantArt is a long-standing online community for artists to share and sell their artwork, including AI-generated art.

Benefits:

  • Large, active user base

  • Opportunities for exposure and feedback

  • Various selling options

Getting Started: Join DeviantArt, upload your art, and use the platform’s selling tools to offer prints and digital downloads. Participate in the community by commenting on and favoriting other artists' works to build your presence【source】.


6. Adobe Stock

Overview: Adobe Stock is a stock photo and digital asset marketplace where artists can sell their images, including AI-generated artwork.

Benefits:

  • Integration with Adobe Creative Cloud

  • Access to a large customer base

  • Royalty payments

Getting Started: Sign up as a contributor, upload your AI art, and start earning royalties for each download. Utilize Adobe’s tools to enhance and prepare your artwork for submission【source】.


7. Shutterstock

Overview: Shutterstock is one of the largest stock photography and digital asset platforms, allowing artists to sell their AI-generated art.

Benefits:

  • Massive global audience

  • Regular payouts

  • Marketing tools for contributors

Getting Started: Become a Shutterstock contributor, upload your artwork, and earn money every time your art is downloaded. Use Shutterstock’s keyword tools to optimize your listings for search.


8. Saatchi Art

Overview: Saatchi Art is a leading online art gallery that offers original art and prints by emerging and established artists.

Benefits:

  • High-end art marketplace

  • Curated collections

  • Global shipping is handled by Saatchi Art

Getting Started: Apply to be an artist on Saatchi Art, upload your work, and set your prices. Saatchi Art handles the logistics of shipping and customer service, allowing you to focus on creating.


9. Fine Art America

Overview: Fine Art America is a print-on-demand service for artists to sell art prints, home decor, and more.

Benefits:

  • Wide range of products

  • Customizable storefront

  • Global reach

Getting Started: Create an account, upload your art, and choose the products to sell your designs on. Fine Art America takes care of the printing and shipping【source】.


10. Mintable

Overview: Mintable is an NFT marketplace where you can sell your AI-generated art as NFTs (non-fungible tokens).

Benefits:

  • Growing interest in NFTs

  • Easy to mint and sell art

  • Decentralized platform

Getting Started: Sign up on Mintable, mint your AI art as NFTs, and list them for sale. Promote your NFTs on social media and within NFT communities to attract buyers【source】.


11. OpenSea

Overview: OpenSea is the largest NFT marketplace, offering a platform for artists to sell digital art and other digital assets.

Benefits:

  • Extensive marketplace

  • High visibility

  • Various payment options

Getting Started: Create an OpenSea account, mint your NFTs, and list your AI art for sale. Use tags and descriptions to make your listings more discoverable【source】.


12. Rarible

Overview: Rarible is a community-owned NFT marketplace where artists can mint, buy, and sell digital art.

Benefits:

  • Decentralized platform

  • Royalty features

  • Active community

Getting Started: Sign up on Rarible, mint your AI art as NFTs, and start selling to collectors. Engage with the community to build your reputation and attract buyers【source】.


13. Foundation

Overview: Foundation is an invite-only NFT marketplace known for high-quality digital art and exclusive drops.

Benefits:

  • Curated platform

  • High-profile buyers

  • Exclusive community

Getting Started: Request an invite to join the Foundation, mint your NFTs, and list your AI art for sale. Utilize the platform’s marketing tools to promote your art【source】.


14. Zazzle

Overview: Zazzle is a print-on-demand marketplace that allows artists to sell designs on a variety of products.

Benefits:

  • Extensive product range

  • Easy customization

  • International customer base

Getting Started: Create a Zazzle store, upload your designs, and select the products you want to sell. Promote your store through social media and other marketing channels.


15. Teespring

Overview: Teespring is a platform that lets creators design and sell custom merchandise without upfront costs.

Benefits:

  • No upfront costs

  • Integrated marketing tools

  • Wide range of products

Getting Started: Set up a Teespring account, create your designs, and promote your store to start selling. Use Teespring’s marketing features to reach a wider audience【source】.


16. Threadless

Overview: Threadless is a print-on-demand service where artists can sell designs on apparel, accessories, and home decor.

Benefits:

  • Artist-friendly platform

  • Community-driven

  • Various product options

Getting Started: Join the Threadless Artist Shops, upload your designs, and start selling to their global audience. Participate in community challenges to gain more exposure.


17. Curioos

Overview: Curioos is an online marketplace for high-quality art prints, catering to a global audience.

Benefits:

  • High-quality prints

  • Global shipping

  • Exclusive artist community

Getting Started: Apply to become a Curioos artist, upload your work, and start selling your art prints. Use Curioos’s marketing tools to promote your art【source】.


18. Displate

Overview: Displate specializes in metal posters and offers a unique platform for artists to sell their designs.

Benefits:

  • Unique product offering

  • Global audience

  • Eco-friendly initiative

Getting Started: Sign up as an artist on Displate, upload your artwork, and start selling metal posters. Promote your Displate shop to attract buyers【source】.


19. ArtPal

Overview: ArtPal is a free gallery for artists to sell their art, including digital and AI-generated pieces.

Benefits:

  • No fees

  • Easy setup

  • Various selling options

Getting Started: Create an ArtPal gallery, upload your art, and choose between print-on-demand or selling originals. Promote your gallery to drive traffic and sales【source】.


20. Pixels

Overview: Pixels is a print-on-demand platform that allows artists to sell their designs on a variety of products.

Benefits:

  • Integration with Fine Art America

  • Wide range of products

  • Easy setup

Getting Started: Create a Pixels account, upload your designs, and choose the products to sell your art on. Use social media and other marketing strategies to promote your Pixels store【source】.


21. Artplode

Overview: Artplode is an online gallery that allows artists to sell their art directly to buyers without paying a commission on sales.

Benefits:

  • No commission fees

  • Direct sales to buyers

  • Support for various art forms

Getting Started: Create an account on Artplode, list your AI art for sale, and set your prices. Use Artplode’s marketing tools to promote your listings【source】.


The digital revolution in the art world has paved the way for artists to monetize their AI-generated creations through various online platforms. By leveraging these 21 platforms, you can showcase your unique AI art to a global audience and generate passive income. Each platform offers distinct features and benefits, catering to different types of art and marketing strategies. Whether you choose traditional art marketplaces or innovative NFT platforms, there’s a perfect fit for your AI art.

By utilizing these platforms effectively, you can reach new audiences, increase your visibility, and maximize your earnings. Start exploring these platforms today, and watch your AI art generate passive income while you focus on creating more incredible pieces.
