How to Earn Up To $300 a Day Watching Videos or Getting Others to Watch Videos with These Apps

SSEGUPost2023-10-31

In the age of digital technology, finding ways to make money online has never been easier. One of the most exciting opportunities is earning by simply watching videos or getting others to watch them. With the rise of video streaming, entertainment, and advertising, several platforms now offer the chance to earn up to $300 a day. If you’re looking for an easy side hustle or even a full-time gig, keep reading to discover the best apps that can help you turn screen time into income.


Why Are Companies Paying You to Watch Videos?

Many companies rely on video marketing to promote their products and services. These videos need viewers, and platforms are willing to pay users like you to engage with their content. Whether it's product advertisements, movie trailers, or promotional clips, companies can gather valuable insights, and in exchange, they offer rewards.

How Does It Work?

Most platforms operate on a simple model:

  • Watch videos: You earn money or points for every video you watch.

  • Refer others: Some apps reward you for getting others to watch videos, boosting your income potential.

  • Complete additional tasks: Many apps offer other activities like answering surveys or providing feedback for extra earnings.

Here are some of the best apps that allow you to earn by watching videos or getting others to watch:

1. Swagbucks

Swagbucks is one of the most popular platforms that lets users earn points, called "SB," for watching videos, shopping online, and completing surveys. These points can be redeemed for gift cards or PayPal cash. You can also refer others to earn more.

  • How much can you earn? While typical earnings range from $5 to $10 per day for casual users, dedicated users can make significantly more, especially by combining video watching with other activities.

  • Referral Bonus: Earn 10% of what your referrals make, giving you an additional source of income.

2. InboxDollars

InboxDollars is similar to Swagbucks, offering cash rewards for watching videos, taking surveys, and shopping online. It’s perfect for those who prefer real cash over points-based systems.

  • How much can you earn? Users can make between $50 and $100 per month just by watching videos. To maximize earnings, combine it with surveys and referrals.

  • Referral Program: Earn 30% of your referral's earnings, which can substantially increase your daily income.

3. MyPoints

MyPoints rewards users for watching short video clips, taking surveys, and shopping online. Like Swagbucks, it offers gift cards and PayPal cash for points earned.

  • How much can you earn? The average user can make up to $150 a month by watching videos and using other features. Additionally, MyPoints has a strong referral system to boost your income.

  • Referral Bonus: Earn a percentage of your referral’s earnings, and sometimes even get bonus points when they sign up.

4. Perk.tv

Perk.tv is an app specifically designed to pay users for watching entertainment and promotional videos. It's available on both Android and iOS, and the points you earn can be redeemed for gift cards or PayPal cash.

  • How much can you earn? Depending on how much time you spend watching videos, you could earn up to $90 per month.

  • Referral Program: Perk.tv offers additional points when you refer friends and family, allowing you to increase your earnings effortlessly.

5. Nielsen Computer & Mobile Panel

Nielsen, a global leader in audience measurement, offers the chance to earn simply by watching videos and browsing the internet. Once you download their app, it monitors your usage habits and rewards you for your participation.

  • How much can you earn? While earnings start modestly, you could potentially make up to $50 annually just by having the app installed. For more dedicated users, watching videos through their network increases your chances of earning bonuses.

  • Referral Program: Occasionally, Nielsen runs referral bonuses for sharing the app with friends.

6. iRazoo

iRazoo is another platform where users are paid to watch videos, play games, and take surveys. The site partners with major brands that need insights into how users engage with their content.

  • How much can you earn? Active users can make up to $50 a month by consistently watching videos. The key to maximizing earnings is watching high-point videos and engaging in other activities like surveys.

  • Referral Program: Earn extra points every time your referrals watch videos or complete other tasks.

7. AppNana

AppNana is a fun app that rewards users for trying out new mobile games and watching videos. You earn "Nanas" (points), which can be redeemed for gift cards or PayPal cash.

  • How much can you earn? While casual users make between $10 and $20 a month, the potential increases when you refer others or complete higher-paying tasks.

  • Referral Bonus: You can earn extra points by getting others to sign up through your referral link.

Tips for Maximizing Your Earnings

  • Stay Consistent: The more videos you watch daily, the more you can earn. Set aside specific times each day to watch videos.

  • Leverage Referrals: Invite friends and family to join, as many apps provide significant bonuses for every new user.

  • Multitask: Watch videos while doing other activities, like household chores or commuting. This way, you maximize time without losing focus on other important tasks.

  • Combine Multiple Apps: Use a combination of apps to diversify your income streams and avoid burnout from one platform.

Conclusion

Earning up to $300 a day by watching videos or getting others to watch may sound too good to be true, but with the right strategy and dedication, it's achievable. Apps like Swagbucks, InboxDollars, and Perk.tv make it easy to earn money in your spare time. Remember, the key to maximizing your earnings is consistency, multitasking, and leveraging referral programs. If you're looking for a fun, flexible, and profitable way to make money online, give these apps a try and start earning today!

By following these tips and making the most out of video-watching apps, you can turn a passive hobby into a consistent source of income.
