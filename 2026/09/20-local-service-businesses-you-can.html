You do not need a loan. You do not need employees.

You need $100, 1 skill, and people who hate doing the task.

In 2027, local service businesses still print cash because people pay for convenience.

The $100 Rule: If you can solve it with your hands + phone, you can start it.

How To Start Any Service With $100

1. $30: Basic tools or supplies

2. $30: Flyers + Google Business Profile

3. $40: Facebook/WhatsApp ads or fuel to get to clients

Goal: First 3 customers = profit

The 20 Service Businesses Under $100

Home + Cleaning Services

1. House Cleaning

Start with: $50 for supplies. Charge $40 to $80 per home

Get clients: Apartments, WhatsApp groups, "first clean 50% off"

2. Window Washing

Start with: $40 squeegee + spray. Charge $10 per window

Get clients: Shops, offices, homes

3. Laundry Pickup

Start with: $0. Use your time. Charge $15 per load

Get clients: Students, busy professionals

4. Organizing + Decluttering

Start with: $0. Charge $25/hour

Get clients: Parents, people moving

Outdoor + Repair Services

5. Lawn Care

Start with: $80 used mower. Charge $30 per yard

Get clients: Door knock in 1 neighborhood

6. Pressure Washing

Start with: $90 rent washer first job. Charge $150 per driveway

Get clients: Before/after photos on Instagram

7. Handyman - Small Jobs

Start with: $100 tool kit. Charge $40/hour

Get clients: "I hang shelves, fix leaks, assemble furniture"

8. Junk Removal

Start with: $0. Use truck + charge $80 per load

Get clients: Post "Free pickup" then charge to dump

Pet + People Services

9. Dog Walking

Start with: $20 leashes. Charge $20 per 30 min walk

Get clients: Flyers at vet, Rover app

10. Pet Sitting

Start with: $0. Charge $30 per day

Get clients: Referrals + Facebook groups

11. Tutoring

Start with: $0. Charge $25 to $50/hour

Get clients: Parents, school boards

12. Personal Chef / Meal Prep

Start with: $80 groceries. Charge $100 for 10 meals

Get clients: Busy families, gym people

Business + Tech Services

13. Social Media Management

Start with: $0. Charge $300/month per business

Get clients: Local shops with bad Instagram

14. Flyer + Poster Design

Start with: $0 Canva. Charge $50 per design

Get clients: Events, shops, restaurants

15. Phone/Computer Setup

Start with: $0. Charge $40 to fix or setup

Get clients: Seniors, small businesses

16. Resume Writing

Start with: $0. Charge $60 per resume

Get clients: LinkedIn, students

Specialty + Niche Services

17. Mobile Car Wash

Start with: $100 bucket + supplies. Charge $40 per car

Get clients: Offices, apartments

18. Event Decor

Start with: $100 balloons. Charge $300 per party

Get clients: Birthdays, weddings on Instagram

19. Photography - Portraits

Start with: $0 phone. Charge $100 for 1 hour shoot

Get clients: Families, small business owners

20. Errand Runner

Start with: $0. Charge $25/hour + costs

Get clients: Busy professionals, elderly

The 7-Day "First $500" Plan

Day 1-2: Pick 1 service. Buy supplies. Make simple flyer on Canva.

Day 3-4: Offer 3 people "50% off first job for testimonial".

Day 5-7: Deliver amazing work. Ask for 5 Google reviews + referrals.

Goal: 5 customers x $100 avg = $500

How To Get Customers For $0

  1. WhatsApp Status + Groups: "I now offer"
  2. Google Business Profile: Free. Ranks you locally
  3. Door knocking: 20 doors = 2 customers
  4. Referrals: "Refer a friend, both get 20% off"

Common Mistakes That Kill You At $0

1. Too many services: Pick 1. Master it

2. Cheap prices: $15 cleaning attracts bad clients. Charge $40+

3. No follow up: "Book next month and save 15%"

4. Waiting for perfect: Start messy. Improve with first 10 clients

FAQ: $100 Businesses

1. Do I need a license?

Start first. Register after you make $1000. Check local rules in Bishkek.

2. What if I have no skills?

Pick "errand runner" or "organizing". You learn on job 1.

3. How fast to $1000/month?

Month 1: $500. Month 2: $1000 with 10 recurring clients.

People do not pay for "cleaning". They pay for "clean house without doing it".

Pick 1 problem. Charge to solve it. Show up on time.

With $100 and 7 days you can have a business that pays you cash.
