Free Data Analytics Courses With Certificates

The good news is that you do not always need to pay for expensive classroom training to start learning data analytics. Several reputable organizations

SSEGUPost2026-08-24

 

Data analytics is one of the most useful digital skills for people looking to improve their CV, change careers, or qualify for entry-level technology and business jobs. Companies in almost every industry use data to understand customers, measure performance, identify problems, forecast trends, and make better decisions.

The good news is that you do not always need to pay for expensive classroom training to start learning data analytics. Several reputable organizations provide free courses, learning resources, and digital credentials. However, there is an important difference between free data analytics training and a free professional certificate. Some platforms let you study at no cost but charge for the final certificate or assessment.

For example, Microsoft Learn provides free self-paced data analytics training, while its professional Power BI Data Analyst certification requires a separate exam fee. 

What Is Data Analytics?

Data analytics involves examining information to discover useful patterns, trends, relationships, and insights. A data analyst may collect, clean, organize, analyze, visualize, and communicate data to help an organization make informed decisions.

Common data analytics tasks include:

  • Collecting and organizing data.
  • Cleaning inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Using spreadsheets and databases.
  • Writing SQL queries.
  • Analyzing trends and patterns.
  • Creating charts and dashboards.
  • Building reports.
  • Communicating findings to decision-makers.

Microsoft describes data analysts as professionals who work with data preparation, cleaning, transformation, modeling, visualization, and reporting. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

Microsoft Learn is one of the best places to start if you want free data analytics training. Microsoft provides self-paced learning paths covering Power BI, data analysis, data preparation, data modeling, reporting, and Microsoft Fabric.

You can continue with additional learning paths covering data preparation, data modeling, and effective reporting. Microsoft provides these resources as part of its data analyst career path. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}

This is particularly useful if you want to develop skills for jobs involving:

  • Power BI.
  • Business intelligence.
  • Reporting.
  • Data analysis.
  • Business reporting.
  • Microsoft data technologies.

2. Microsoft Power BI Training

Power BI is an important business intelligence tool used to transform data into interactive reports and dashboards. Learning Power BI can therefore be a useful addition to a data analytics CV.

Microsoft's learning resources cover areas such as data preparation, data modeling, visualization, reports, and analytics. The official Microsoft data analyst certification also focuses on preparing, modeling, visualizing, and analyzing data with Power BI. :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}

One advantage of learning Power BI is that you can demonstrate your skills through practical projects. Instead of simply saying that you know Power BI, you can create dashboards showing sales, expenses, website traffic, inventory, or other datasets.

The current Google Skills listing describes the program as a professional certificate and says learners can gain hands-on experience with SQL, Tableau, spreadsheets, Python, and R. :contentReference[oaicite:7]{index=7}

  • Data visualization.

6. IBM Data Analytics Learning

IBM provides data analytics training through its learning ecosystem, including courses and credentials covering data, AI, cloud, automation, and related technologies. IBM says its training platform offers thousands of courses, learning paths, certifications, and digital badges. :contentReference[oaicite:11]{index=11}

IBM SkillsBuild is particularly useful for learners looking for free educational resources and shareable digital credentials. IBM describes SkillsBuild as a free learning platform offering courses in areas including data analytics, artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, technical support, and project management. :contentReference[oaicite:12]{index=12}

7. Free Data Visualization Courses

Data visualization is a major part of data analytics. It is not enough to analyze numbers if you cannot explain what they mean to other people.

Learn how to create:

  • Bar charts.
  • Line charts.
  • Pie charts where appropriate.
  • Interactive dashboards.
  • Performance reports.
  • Data storytelling presentations.

Microsoft Power BI provides one route into data visualization, while Google and other learning platforms provide additional training in tools such as Tableau and cloud-based analytics. :contentReference[oaicite:13]{index=13}

8. Free SQL Courses

SQL is one of the most important skills for aspiring data analysts. It allows you to retrieve and manipulate information stored in relational databases.

Beginners should learn concepts such as:

  • SELECT statements.
  • WHERE conditions.
  • Sorting and filtering.
  • GROUP BY.
  • Aggregate functions.
  • JOIN operations.
  • Subqueries.
  • Data modification.

Google's Data Analytics Certificate includes SQL, while other training providers also offer dedicated SQL courses. :contentReference[oaicite:14]{index=14}

If you are applying for data analyst positions, SQL is one of the skills worth highlighting prominently on your CV.

9. Free Excel and Spreadsheet Training

You do not need advanced programming skills to begin learning data analytics. Spreadsheet skills are an excellent starting point.

Focus on:

  • Sorting and filtering.
  • Formulas.
  • Functions.
  • Pivot tables.
  • Charts.
  • Data cleaning.
  • Lookup functions.
  • Conditional formatting.

Once you are comfortable with spreadsheets, moving into SQL, Power BI, Python, or other analytics tools becomes easier.

10. Free Python Courses for Data Analytics

Python is widely used for data analysis, automation, machine learning, and data science. It is therefore a valuable skill for anyone who wants to move beyond basic spreadsheet analysis.

Start with:

  • Python syntax.
  • Variables and data types.
  • Functions.
  • Lists and dictionaries.
  • Loops.
  • Data manipulation.
  • Libraries such as pandas.
  • Basic data visualization.

The Google Data Analytics Certificate currently includes Python alongside SQL, R, Tableau, and spreadsheet-based analytics. :contentReference[oaicite:15]{index=15}

What Should a Beginner Learn First?

If you have never studied data analytics before, do not try to learn everything simultaneously.

A practical learning sequence is:

  1. Excel or spreadsheets: Learn how to organize and analyze basic datasets.
  2. SQL: Learn how to retrieve information from databases.
  3. Power BI or Tableau: Learn how to create dashboards and visualizations.
  4. Statistics: Understand averages, percentages, distributions, and basic statistical concepts.
  5. Python: Add programming and advanced analysis skills.
  6. Portfolio projects: Apply everything you have learned.

Do Free Data Analytics Courses Include Certificates?

This depends on the platform.

Some providers offer completely free courses with digital badges or certificates. Others provide free learning materials but charge for a certificate, exam, or subscription.

Microsoft Learn, for example, provides free training, but its Power BI Data Analyst Associate is a separate professional certification that currently requires a paid exam. Microsoft lists the exam price as $165 USD, with the actual price varying by country or region. :contentReference[oaicite:16]{index=16}

Similarly, Google's professional data analytics certificates currently require paid access unless you qualify for a specific free-access program or other available arrangement. :contentReference[oaicite:17]{index=17}

Always check the current certificate conditions before registering.

How to Build a Data Analytics Portfolio

A certificate can show that you completed training, but a portfolio can show what you can actually do.

Choose public datasets and create practical projects such as:

  • Monthly sales analysis.
  • Customer behavior analysis.
  • Marketing campaign performance.
  • Personal finance dashboard.
  • Inventory analysis.
  • Website traffic report.
  • Employee performance analysis.
  • Transportation data analysis.

For each project, explain the problem, the data you used, how you cleaned and analyzed it, the visualizations you created, and the conclusions you reached.

How to Add Data Analytics Courses to Your CV

Create a section titled Certifications and Professional Training or Data Analytics Training.

Include the provider, exact course or credential name, and completion date where appropriate.

You can also create a Technical Skills section containing relevant skills such as:

  • Microsoft Excel.
  • SQL.
  • Power BI.
  • Tableau.
  • Python.
  • R.
  • Data visualization.
  • Data cleaning.
  • Data modeling.

Only list skills you can actually demonstrate in an interview or practical assessment.

Can a Data Analytics Certificate Get You a Job?

A certificate cannot guarantee employment. Employers usually consider a combination of education, technical ability, experience, projects, communication, and interview performance.

However, a relevant certificate can help demonstrate that you have deliberately developed skills for the field. Google's data analytics program, for example, is specifically designed around job-ready analytics skills and includes practical tools and a portfolio-style capstone. 

The strongest combination is therefore:

Course + certificate or credential + practical project + strong CV + interview preparation.

Free data analytics courses can be an excellent starting point for anyone who wants to develop valuable digital skills without immediately spending large amounts of money. Microsoft Learn provides free Power BI and data analytics training, while IBM SkillsBuild offers free learning and digital credentials in data analytics and related technology areas. :contentReference[oaicite:19]{index=19}

Google also provides several data analytics certificate programs, although its current professional certificate offerings are not universally free, so learners should check the latest pricing and eligibility for free-access programs. :contentReference[oaicite:20]{index=20}

If you are starting from zero, focus on spreadsheets, SQL, data visualization, statistics, and eventually Python. Most importantly, build projects while you learn. A portfolio showing real analysis can make your CV much more convincing than a long list of unrelated certificates.

Whether your goal is to become a junior data analyst, business intelligence analyst, reporting specialist, marketing analyst, data technician, or cloud data analyst, consistent learning and practical experience can help you move closer to your target career.
