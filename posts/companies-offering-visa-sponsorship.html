Companies Offering Visa Sponsorship Jobs for International Workers

This guide highlights companies worth researching when looking for visa sponsorship jobs and explains how international applicants can identify genuin

SSEGUPost2026-08-21

 

For international job seekers, finding an employer willing to sponsor a work visa can make the difference between simply finding a job abroad and having a realistic pathway to relocate. Many multinational companies regularly recruit talent from different countries, particularly in technology, engineering, healthcare, finance, consulting, logistics, and other specialized fields.

However, it is important to understand that a company being known for hiring international workers does not mean every vacancy includes visa sponsorship. Sponsorship depends on the country, position, salary, candidate's qualifications, and the employer's immigration requirements. In the UK, for example, employers generally need a sponsor licence to employ eligible workers from outside the country, and sponsorship itself does not guarantee that the worker will receive a visa. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

This guide highlights companies worth researching when looking for visa sponsorship jobs and explains how international applicants can identify genuine opportunities.

What Does Visa Sponsorship Mean?

Visa sponsorship generally means an employer supports a foreign worker's application for the immigration permission needed to work legally in a particular country.

Depending on the destination, the employer may need to:

  • Hold an approved sponsor licence or recognition.
  • Offer an eligible position.
  • Meet minimum salary requirements.
  • Provide an employment contract.
  • Submit sponsorship or immigration documentation.
  • Comply with government reporting requirements.

The exact process differs from one country to another. In the Netherlands, for example, an employer must be an IND-recognized sponsor for the highly skilled migrant route, and the employee must meet income and other requirements. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

Potential career areas include:

  • Software engineering.
  • Cloud and Azure.
  • Artificial intelligence.
  • Cybersecurity.
  • Data science.
  • Product management.
  • Technical consulting.

2. Google

Google recruits professionals across technology, advertising, cloud computing, artificial intelligence, data, product management, sales, and operations.

For international applicants, the best strategy is to search Google's official careers portal for specific countries and positions. Do not assume that every international vacancy offers sponsorship. Look for information about relocation, work authorization, or immigration support during the recruitment process.

3. Amazon

Amazon has a large international workforce covering ecommerce, cloud computing, logistics, advertising, technology, and corporate services.

Amazon Web Services also creates opportunities for professionals with cloud, software, infrastructure, cybersecurity, and data expertise.

International candidates should search for jobs that specifically match their qualifications and carefully review the location and work-authorization requirements listed in each vacancy.

4. Apple

Apple recruits professionals in software engineering, hardware engineering, product development, operations, retail, supply chain, design, finance, and other areas.

Specialized technical roles can be particularly relevant to international applicants because companies may seek candidates with experience that is difficult to find in local talent pools.

5. Meta

Meta, the company behind Facebook, Instagram, and WhatsApp, recruits professionals in software engineering, artificial intelligence, data science, product management, cybersecurity, design, and business operations.

Applicants should search by location and carefully check the work authorization requirements for each vacancy.

6. Booking.com

Booking.com is headquartered in Amsterdam and has a highly international workforce. Technology, data, product, engineering, and commercial roles can be particularly relevant for professionals interested in working in the Netherlands.

For applicants targeting the Netherlands, the key question is not simply whether a company is large or international. The employer must be able to use the relevant immigration route. The IND maintains a public register where applicants can verify whether an organization is a recognized sponsor. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}

7. ING

ING is a major international banking group with headquarters in the Netherlands. Potential opportunities can include technology, data, finance, risk management, compliance, cybersecurity, and other specialized functions.

International finance professionals should check individual vacancies rather than assuming that every ING position supports immigration sponsorship.

8. Adyen

Adyen is an international financial technology company headquartered in Amsterdam. Its business depends heavily on technology, payments infrastructure, data, cybersecurity, and product development.

Professionals with backgrounds in software engineering, data, product management, payments, and technology can investigate opportunities through the company's official careers channels.

9. Deloitte

Deloitte operates internationally and recruits professionals in consulting, technology, cybersecurity, finance, accounting, risk, data, and business transformation.

International applicants should focus on specialist positions where their qualifications and experience match the employer's requirements.

10. KPMG

KPMG is another global professional-services company that recruits across accounting, auditing, consulting, technology, risk, tax, cybersecurity, and data-related fields.

Visa sponsorship policies can differ between offices and countries, so candidates should confirm eligibility with the specific vacancy and local recruitment team.

11. PwC

PwC has offices across numerous countries and recruits professionals in consulting, accounting, technology, cybersecurity, finance, tax, and business services.

Applicants with internationally recognized qualifications and experience can search for positions that specifically accept candidates requiring work authorization.

12. EY

EY offers career opportunities in consulting, technology, accounting, assurance, tax, cybersecurity, data, and financial services.

Because immigration rules vary by country, candidates should check the individual office and vacancy before assuming sponsorship is available.

13. Shell

Shell is a global energy company with opportunities spanning engineering, technology, finance, project management, data, operations, and business services.

Specialized engineering and technical professionals may find international opportunities particularly relevant because of the global nature of the energy industry.

14. Heineken

Heineken is an international consumer-goods company headquartered in the Netherlands. Its global operations create opportunities in supply chain, technology, marketing, finance, manufacturing, engineering, and management.

International applicants interested in the Netherlands should verify the company's current sponsor status through the official IND register when sponsorship is required. The register is updated monthly. :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}

15. ASML

ASML is a major Dutch technology company specializing in semiconductor manufacturing equipment. Its highly specialized workforce includes engineers, software developers, physicists, technicians, data professionals, and other technical specialists.

For international technology and engineering professionals, companies working in advanced semiconductor technology can be worth researching because specialized skills are central to their operations.

16. Siemens

Siemens is a global engineering and technology company with operations in industrial automation, infrastructure, digital industries, healthcare technology, and energy-related solutions.

Engineers, software professionals, project managers, and technical specialists can search its international career opportunities.

17. Bosch

Bosch operates internationally across automotive technology, industrial technology, consumer products, energy, and building technology.

Potential opportunities include engineering, software development, manufacturing, research, data, electronics, and technical project management.

18. Accenture

Accenture recruits professionals across consulting, technology, cloud computing, cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, data, and business transformation.

Its international operations make it another company worth investigating when searching for global career opportunities.

Which Jobs Are Most Likely to Offer Sponsorship?

Not every occupation has the same sponsorship potential. Employers are more likely to consider international recruitment when they need specialized skills or have difficulty finding suitable candidates locally.

Fields worth targeting include:

  • Software engineering.
  • Artificial intelligence.
  • Data science.
  • Cybersecurity.
  • Cloud computing.
  • Engineering.
  • Healthcare.
  • Research.
  • Specialized finance.
  • Technology consulting.
  • Advanced manufacturing.

How to Check Whether a Company Can Sponsor You

Do not rely solely on lists published on blogs or social media. Sponsorship status can change, and an approved sponsor may not sponsor every position.

For the Netherlands, the IND provides an official public register of recognized sponsors. You can use it to verify whether a company has recognized sponsor status. :contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5}

The IND explains that recognized sponsorship is required for certain routes, including the highly skilled migrant route. :contentReference[oaicite:6]{index=6}

For the UK, employers generally need a sponsor licence to employ eligible workers from outside the country. :contentReference[oaicite:7]{index=7}

How to Search for Sponsorship Jobs

When searching company career pages, use keywords such as:

  • Visa sponsorship.
  • Work permit sponsorship.
  • Relocation assistance.
  • International applicants.
  • Global mobility.
  • Immigration support.
  • Skilled worker sponsorship.

You can also search for a specific company followed by your occupation, such as “software engineer visa sponsorship” or “data scientist relocation opportunity.”

How to Improve Your Chances

International sponsorship is competitive, so your application should clearly demonstrate why an employer should consider you.

  • Build skills in an in-demand occupation.
  • Gain relevant professional experience.
  • Earn recognized qualifications.
  • Create a strong CV.
  • Build a professional portfolio.
  • Improve your interview skills.
  • Apply for positions that match your experience.
  • Research immigration requirements before applying.

Be Careful of Visa Sponsorship Scams

International job seekers should be especially careful with offers that promise guaranteed employment or guaranteed visa approval.

A legitimate employer should provide a professional recruitment process and a clear job description. Be suspicious if someone asks you to pay large amounts of money simply to receive a job offer or promises that your visa is guaranteed.

Remember that even an employer with permission to sponsor workers cannot guarantee that an immigration authority will approve your application. The UK government specifically states that sponsorship does not guarantee a worker will receive a visa. :contentReference[oaicite:8]{index=8}

Companies such as Microsoft, Google, Amazon, Apple, Meta, Booking.com, ING, Adyen, Deloitte, KPMG, PwC, EY, Shell, Heineken, ASML, Siemens, Bosch, and Accenture are worth researching for international career opportunities because of their global operations and need for specialized talent.

However, the most important rule is to verify every vacancy individually. A company may be able to sponsor workers without offering sponsorship for every job. Immigration requirements also differ between countries.

If you are an international applicant, concentrate on occupations where your skills are in demand, search the official career pages of multinational companies, verify sponsorship eligibility through government sources where possible, and never pay an unofficial recruiter for a supposedly guaranteed visa.

With the right qualifications, targeted applications, and careful research, international workers can find legitimate employers that may support their move into a new country and a new career.
