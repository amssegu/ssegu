Best Websites to Find Visa Sponsorship Jobs and Free Courses

The challenge is knowing which websites are real and which are scams. This guide gives you the best websites to find visa sponsorship jobs and free co

SSEGUPost2026-10-03

 

Finding a job abroad with visa sponsorship and learning new skills for free used to take months of research. In 2026, you can do both online from your phone.

The challenge is knowing which websites are real and which are scams. This guide gives you the best websites to find visa sponsorship jobs and free courses with certificates that actually help you get hired.

Bookmark this page. These are the official, trusted sites recruiters and governments use.

Part 1: Best Websites to Find Visa Sponsorship Jobs

These sites list real employers who are legally allowed to sponsor foreign workers.

1. Government Job Portals

Government sites are the safest place to start. Jobs here are from approved employers.

Canada Job Bank: jobbank.gc.ca

Filter "LMIA available" to see jobs with sponsorship. High demand in farming, healthcare, construction, and hospitality.

UK Find a Job + UKVI Sponsor List: findajob.dwp.gov.uk and gov.uk/find-a-licensed-sponsor

Only apply to companies on the UKVI list. They can legally sponsor Skilled Worker visas.

Australia JobActive and Seek: jobsearch.gov.au and seek.com.au

Search "482 visa sponsorship". Australia sponsors nurses, chefs, mechanics, and farm workers.

USA SeasonalJobs.dol.gov

Official US government site for H-2A farm and H-2B seasonal hotel jobs with sponsorship.

Germany Make it in Germany: make-it-in-germany.com

Lists jobs for skilled workers and the new Opportunity Card. Nursing and engineering roles are open.

2. Global Job Boards With Visa Filters

These sites let you filter for "visa sponsorship" or "relocation".

Indeed.com

Search "visa sponsorship" + job title + country. Example: "visa sponsorship nurse UK"

LinkedIn Jobs: linkedin.com/jobs

Use filters: "Easy Apply" and keywords "visa sponsorship". Connect with recruiters directly.

Glassdoor

See salary, reviews, and if the company has sponsored before.

3. Websites That Track H-1B and Skilled Sponsors

MyVisaJobs.com

Database of US companies that sponsored H-1B and green cards. See who hired foreigners last year.

H1BGrader.com

Check salary and approval rates for US tech sponsors.

4. Gulf and Middle East Job Sites

Bayt.com

Largest job site for UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar. Most jobs include visa, housing, and flights.

Gulftalent.com

Professional jobs in finance, engineering, and hospitality with sponsorship.

5. Industry-Specific Job Boards

Hospitality: hcareers.com, catererglobal.com

Healthcare: nhscareers.nhs.uk for UK, healthforceontario.ca for Canada

Agriculture: agrirecruit.ca for Canada farms

Teaching: tefl.com, searchassociates.com

Example: Canada + Healthcare

Take "Healthcare Assistant" on Alison + "Communication" on LinkedIn Learning

Step 3: Find jobs on government sites

Go to Canada Job Bank and search "caregiver LMIA"

Step 4: Apply with new certificate

Add certificate to resume and LinkedIn. Apply to 10 jobs per week.

How to Avoid Scams on These Websites

Even on good sites, scammers post fake jobs.

1. Never pay: Real jobs do not ask for money. Government sites are free.

2. Check company on sponsor list: For UK and Australia, verify on government list.

3. Use company email: Real HR uses @company.com, not @gmail.com

4. Do video interview: No interview = red flag.

5. Free courses are free: If a site asks you to pay before watching, leave.

FAQ: Websites for Jobs and Courses

1. Are these websites really free?

Yes. Government job boards and the courses listed are free. You may pay for a printed certificate.

2. Which site is best for beginners?

For jobs: Job Bank Canada and LinkedIn. For courses: Google Digital Garage and Microsoft Learn.

3. Can I get a job with only a free certificate?

Yes for entry jobs. Combine certificate + 1 project + applications.

4. Do I need English for these sites?

Most job and course sites are in English. Some have other languages.

5. How do I know if a job on LinkedIn offers sponsorship?

Use keywords: "visa sponsorship", "work visa", "relocation assistance" in search.
